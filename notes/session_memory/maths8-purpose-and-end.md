The end of Maths 8 (stated by the human 2026-10-25; only half-encoded in the repo at the time, now written into QUESTION.md's "Why" section): derive the structure of reality from one self-registering act with no imported background, honestly enough to separate what is **forced** from what is **merely read** — so the collapse of the old divisions yields real **handles** on what we perceive as the substance of the universe. The sentiment matters more than the exact nouns: a world of more abundance and less suffering; the resources a life rests on (high-quality food, clean water, power, clean air) made ubiquitous and widely shared; stewardship and cooperation with the ecosystem, not extraction; the **facilitation** of agency and flourishing, never their definition or enforcement. Reach is an **option, not a demand**: near-term, better handles bring exploration and resources now beyond grasp within reach; long-term, the ability to leave the planet is a survival condition. Concretely: understand gravity, magnetism, mass — why QFT and GR don't reconcile and how they in fact can.

Understanding is the method; what we can *do* with it is the point. QUESTION.md defines *done* as "a handle you can turn — computable and manipulable, precise enough to predict or enable something new; explanation that cannot be turned is not finished." Reaching the irreducible roots (the 3 Planck anchors, K2, N) is where *derivation* bottoms out — a stopping point to recognize honestly, **not** the win condition.

**Why:** a prior reading (this session, before correction) inverted means and end — it treated reaching/recognizing the irreducible roots as the goal, because it quoted QUESTION.md's "done" section but skipped the handle clause. Admiring a clean forced-vs-read separation, carried down to tidy roots, with nothing ever turned, is itself the ceremony the project explicitly threw out (see [[README]] / the Maths 8 reset). The humanitarian and expansionist end is what keeps the honesty in service of something.

**How to apply:** judge work here by whether it moves toward a turnable handle, not by how cleanly it grades the floor. Keep the honesty discipline (it is the right method) but never let it become the destination.
