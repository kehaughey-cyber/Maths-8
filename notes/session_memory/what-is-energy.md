**What is energy (session reading, grades below).** **CORRECTION (human):** change ≠ registration — *registration is the **record** of the change, not the change* (announcer vs the game; conflating them is the category error one layer down). So: **energy is the capacity to cause the *change itself*** (the "ability to do work / cause change"). Registration is the record of that change, and the **rate of registration tracks the change but is not it**. Energy is a **reading (a "what"), conjugate to the progressions (the clocks)**, not a fundamental coordinate; it lives on the event, not on the record.

Because there are **three clocks** (radit / walk / propagation), the familiar energy *types* are registration counted on different clocks [frame/lead]:
- radiant (light) → propagation-clock registration, the massless band (the wave moving);
- electrical → propagation-clock registration of the charge/deficit (current = deficit in transit);
- thermal → **disordered** registration spread across the web (relaxation toward Haar — the structural arrow, F2/F3);
- chemical → stored **re-co-definition** of the relational web (bonds = frozen co-definition cost);
- nuclear → deep binding: the **confined recursion's self-registration** (the proton lesson).
So: in-transit (propagation) = kinetic/radiant/electrical; disordered = thermal; stored-as-binding (live web) = chemical/nuclear. One thing: capacity for registered change.

**The clock-invariant core [grounded]:** energy is conjugate to time, and there are three times, so there are three conjugate rates — but the invariant across them is the **2π-completion**: `E·T = h`, the rate of completing one registration, the same however clocked (the Compton–Bekenstein keystone in native clothing). **Mass (rest energy) = the standing / self face of this capacity** — the rate at which a bound recursion registers itself; E=mc² reads it, c² converting walk/propagation units.
