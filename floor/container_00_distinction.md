# Container 00 — the distinction · statement

**Capped, and graded per claim.** The floor: rung 0, rests on nothing. Rebuilt 2026-08-19
after a floor correction — the first version was faithful to maths 4 and carried three
buried errors (dyadic, negation-founded, frozen). Each claim below is graded **DERIVED**
(recomputed and kernel-checked here), **POSTULATE** (stated working hypothesis, not proven),
or **OWED** (deferred to a later container). The Lean axiom report is the deliverable for the
derived half; the check is `lean/Container00/Distinction.lean`.

## DERIVED — checked here, zero axioms

**The three existential states (0.2).** A thing is `definedExistent`, `undefinedExistent`, or
`nonExistent`. These are distinct; **undefined is not non-existent** (it exists, and can be
perceived and later defined); and exactly the two *existent* states are perceivable —
non-existence cannot be perceived or registered. Constructive proofs, **no axioms**.

**A distinction carries no is-not (0.3).** Within existence, a distinction marks part of a
base as *defined* (the state) and leaves the rest *undefined-existent* (the residual). Both
are selected **positively** — `= defined`, `= undefined` — never as `not state`. There is no
negation on the floor. **No axioms.**

**Proposition 1, the two poles (0.5).** A distinction requires *both* a defined and an
undefined thing: a wholly-defined base leaves no residual to be distinguished from, and a
wholly-undefined base has no state. Neither is a distinction. The bound needs both sides, both
existent. **No axioms.**

## POSTULATE — stated, held, not proven

**Registration 1 is the existence bound (0.1).** All-that-is, bounded against non-existence.
Existence is a distinction only against non-existence — but non-existence is the *counterpoint*,
not a thing to subdivide or perceive; "0" is that non-existence, and it does not exist. Every
later registration divides the residual *inside* all-that-is, never this bound. Stated as the
founding act; not derived.

**The triad (0.4).** A distinction is registered by **three observers — two within, one
without** — who observe, perceive, and agree; their live agreement *is* the artifact: the
registration. The observers are the objects; observation is the act (perceive + register); the
registration is the record — and the observers are **not** the registration, they produce it.
Two poles alone are bare polarity; the third makes the difference a *registered* distinction.
Encoded as a structure that *requires* three observers — but a structure existing is not a
proof it is necessary.

**Proposition 1, the third (0.6).** The two poles co-defining is polarity; it becomes a
registered distinction only with the perceiving *without*. "Neither can exist without
perception." **This is Kevin's working hypothesis**: that all three observers are present
already at registration 1, by instinct, with no handle yet to derive it. Formalising is itself
a handle — and the structure does **not** force the third, so it is assumed, and this says so.
Open to disproof; the interest is the truth, not the hypothesis.

## OWED — deferred, not smuggled here

**Co-definition by adjacency, and the count 12 → the icosahedron.** Registrations are
co-defined by their *adjacent* neighbours, live, propagating stepwise — that is container 01's
concern, foreshadowed here. The claim that adjacency forces **12** (and thus the icosahedron)
is real and owed, and it lands at container 05/06. It is deliberately **not** built into the
floor, so it isn't smuggled before it is earned.

## Not claimed

No time, no space, no number, no geometry. The finite field of the model is a computational
witness, not a claim that all-that-is is finite. And the *reading* — that reality is founded
this way — is postulate-tier throughout; this container establishes only what is marked
DERIVED, and names the rest.
