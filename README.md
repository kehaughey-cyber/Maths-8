# Maths 8

One recursive act — observation and being — and how everything we measure is a reading of it.

**Start with [`QUESTION.md`](QUESTION.md)** — the single locked question and the method. Then [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for how to work here, and [`floor/`](floor/) for the inherited, immutable base.

## Why this project exists (and what changed)

Maths 5/6/7 established a large, forced **floor**: the carrier 2I / 600-cell, the triad and recursive registration, the Galois mass structure, the gauge gradings, the Born overlap, the Regge–Einstein curvature. That floor is kept. What is dropped is the accumulated *frontier* apparatus of Maths 7 — the Q001 dynamical candidate-action zoo, the atomic-numerics N-series, the stability machinery. Those were motion around the question, not toward it.

Maths 8 re-roots on the floor and points everything at the one question, judged only by: **is the math true, and does it match the universe.**

## Organization (deliberately minimal)

- `QUESTION.md` — the contract.
- `CLAUDE.md` — operating instructions (the principle, not a bureaucracy).
- `floor/` — the inherited base: the Maths 5 and Maths 6 spines, the field equations, and the first containers (00 distinction / 01 recursive registration / 02 registration event), each with its source path and sha256 in `floor/PROVENANCE.md`. The full Maths 5/6/7 trees remain at their original locations for deep reference.
- Work is organized around the three touches in `QUESTION.md` (amplitude/QIT, geometry/GR, the force-gradings), each traced inward to the one act.
