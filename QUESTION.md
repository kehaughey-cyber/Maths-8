# The question

> **Derive how amplitude (Hilbert), geometry (spacetime), probability (the overlap), and the force-gradings each arise as readings of the one recursive act of observation and being — in a form that reproduces what we have already touched in QIT, QFT and GR, and yields manipulable handles on mass, space, time, and the forces.**

Locked 2026-10-25. This replaces the older bottom-up objective (particle content → atoms). It is the single contract for Maths 8.

## What the question refuses to assume

QFT imports Hilbert space; GR imports spacetime; QM imports probability. All three take their container as **pre-existent**. This project does not. Space, time, number, geometry, amplitude, probability are **readings** of one recursive act — observation and being — at different extents of coordination (triad / cell / collective; see `floor/`). There is no background. There is therefore nothing to *unify* and nothing to *reconcile*: unification is a **consequence** of there being one act, never a goal. Any sentence that says "reconcile X and Y" or "unify the N forces" has smuggled the duality back in, and is wrong.

## The three touches already made (the threads to trace from)

We have already made contact with each reading at a point on the floor. The work is to trace each inward to the one act, without stopping at any wall:

- **amplitude / QIT** — the native overlap `|⟨a,b⟩|²`, the Born measure, derived cold from the quaternion product (floor F1/55; the unique SO(4)-invariant positive form is norm²).
- **geometry / GR** — curvature as the density deficit, the Regge–Einstein relation, deficit ⟷ curvature, the 4π double cover (floor 13–21, 37–54).
- **the forces** — not four things to glue, but **gradings and curvatures of the one act**: su(3)×su(2)×u(1) as colour/flavour/hypercharge gradings; gravity as torsion/curvature (floor 28–31, 50–58). Already shown as readings, not forces to unify.

Probability sits with amplitude; mass, space, time are the handles the readings must deliver.

## The only test

Not whether it looks like the Standard Model. Not convention, not dogma, not what a previous session was willing to assert. Two questions, nothing else:

1. **Is the math true?** (exact where structural; derivation, not assertion)
2. **Does it match the universe?** (observation decides; no fitting, ever)

The SM, GR and QM are **comparison surfaces in the dock**, not the bench. A result that is true and matches, but looks unconventional, stands. A result that looks respectable but is false or doesn't match, falls.

## What "done" means

A reading is understood only when it is a **handle** — something computable and manipulable, precise enough to (a) reproduce the known contact and (b) predict or enable something new. Explanation that cannot be turned is not finished.
