# Map — degrees of freedom of the one registration

The independent coordinates of the state, the three progressions (clocks) they advance on, and the derived
readings that are *not* axes. Companion to `mass_requirements.md`.

**Principle.** A degree of freedom is a coordinate you can set **independently**, not every name you can read
off the state. Most physical quantities (mass, charge, energy, dimension) are *readings* — functions across
the coordinates — not coordinates. **"Dynamics" is not an axis**; it is evolution along a clock.

## The three clocks (progressions — how coordinates advance; not axes)

| clock | advance of | period | floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Radit** | the 2π cycle (coord 4) | 12 radits = 2π | 32 |
| **Walk** | the tick C's Coxeter march through the carrier (coord 3) | order 30 | 64 |
| **Propagation** | a change of registration hopping on the adjacency (coord 2) | causal depth ≤ 5 (diameter) | 46 |

The floor **keeps these apart** ("keep C out of propagation"). Any "rate" (including energy) must name its
clock, or be the clock-**invariant** (the 2π-completion, E·T = h).
