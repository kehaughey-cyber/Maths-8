# One gram of carbon-12, read in four languages, with the subordinate understandings (2026-10-29)

Consolidates `lepton_ladder.md`, `full_ladder.md`, `chemical_ladder.md`, `carbon_one_gram.md`, `carbon_gram_refinements.md`, `four_languages_ladder.md`, `colour_column.md` and the nucleon row. Nothing here is new computation; every number is in those files or their `.out` files. Where a statement corrects an earlier one, it says so.

## The common language
Everything is written as a count: cycles = R·E/ħc, which is action over ħ (phase accumulated across a size at an energy); 1 cycle = 2π nats = 12 radits = 30 ticks = 9.0647 bits. The counts are all real; each language is one reading of the same rung. Several table entries are identities of the definitions (marked **id**); those are not findings.

## The four languages, one rung read four ways

### 1. Energy and size (cycles = R·E/ħc)
| rung | p12 above l_P | E (eV) | cycles |
|---|---|---|---|
| τ / μ / e / p (Compton) | 17.45 / 18.59 / 20.74 / 17.71 | mc² | 1 each (**id**) |
| ¹²C nucleus (r_ch, B) | 18.70 | 9.2162e7 | 1.1537 |
| H atom | 22.72 | 13.606 | 0.003649 |
| C valence | 23.03 | 11.261 | 0.006639 |
| C–C bond | 23.15 | 3.686 | 0.002885 |
| gram (sphere radius) | 30.02 | 5.61e32 | 1.160e37 |

Subordinate understandings
- Bands: nuclear Compton lengths 15.51–17.71 p12; chemical level 22.60–23.75 p12 (diatomic cycles 0.0202–1.7404 × α/2, solids 0.2874–1.4104 × α/2); a 1.9-power-of-12 jump from nuclei to the chemical shelf and a 6.3 jump to the gram, nothing populated between. Step ratios in the lepton/quark/nuclear ladders are not powers of 12 or φ; the few small phase residuals are at chance level for the uncertainties.
- Nucleus: cycles grow as A^(4/3) (B ∝ A, r ∝ A^(1/3)): ⁴He 0.2403, ¹²C 1.1537, ⁵⁶Fe 9.324, ²⁰⁸Pb 45.62 (radii for He/Fe/Pb recalled, not sourced). The carbon value is a point on a rising curve. Constant across nuclei: about 0.05 cycles per nucleon (saturation, B/A ≈ 8 MeV, r₀ ≈ 1.2 fm).
- Nucleon (the same-size object inside every nucleus): r_p/ƛ_p = 3.9977 ± 0.0030 (CODATA 2022 r_p = 0.84075(64) fm), within 0.76σ of 4. Observed, not derived.
- Robustness: radius convention ±0.1–0.6% on nuclear cycles; lattice constant 1e-4 on volume; diamond→graphite ×1.157 on the gram's radius (+0.059 p12). No order-of-magnitude or p12 statement moves at the second decimal. Printed digits beyond 3–5 figures are the inputs' digits, not accuracy.

### 2. Mass (gravitational coupling (m/m_P)² = pair-binding cycles of gravity)
| rung | m/m_e | (m/m_P)² |
|---|---|---|
| e | 1 | 1.75e-45 |
| p | 1836.15 | 5.91e-39 |
| ¹²C nucleus | 21868.7 | 8.38e-37 |
| gram | 1.0978e27 | 2.111e9 |

Subordinate understandings
- The coupling passes 1 at the Planck mass, between the nucleus and the gram; the gram is the first rung where it is order one or larger (M/m_P = 45946.7).
- Mass ladder: ν₃(min, oscillations) 0.0503021 eV → e is 10158603 (log₁₂ 6.49), the largest step and the least constrained (neutrino rows are lower bounds); e→t is 337711 (log₁₂ 5.12). Koide Q(e,μ,τ) − 2/3 = −2.2e-6 with PDG τ. Quark rows are scheme-dependent.
- Quarks in the atom: 18u + 18d (MS-bar 2 GeV) = 123.48 MeV = 1.105% of the atom's rest energy; ±2% statistical (PDG ±0.07 MeV each), ~±20% with the scale convention (1.35% at 1 GeV, 1.02% at 3 GeV, a one-loop illustration). About 99% is nucleon mass, not bare quark mass.

### 3. Charge (Coulomb cycles αQ² = pair-binding cycles of EM; and colour)
| rung | Q | αQ² | E/E_C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compton rungs | 1 | 0.0072974 | 137.036 (**id**, = 1/α) |
| ¹²C nucleus | 6 | 0.2627 | 4.392 |
| H atom | 1 | 0.0072974 | 0.5 (**id**, virial) |
| C valence | 1.82 | 0.02416 | 0.2748 |
| gram | 0 net | – | – |

Subordinate understandings
- Gram: positive charge 6N e = 48242.67 C; all charges 12N e = 96485.33 C (the Faraday constant, since 12N = 1 mol); net 0. The unscreened Coulomb energy of the positive charge alone at the gram's radius is 5.70e7 × Mc².
- Colour column (strong, with C_F = 4/3): α_g1(Q²=0) = π (Deur–Brodsky–de Téramond 2016, eq. 4.4), measured and near-flat at low Q²; α_V(0) = 0.42 ± 0.03; α_s(m_Z²) = 0.1180 ± 0.0009. Colour pair-binding cycles C_F·α: 4.1888 (g1) or 0.56 (V). ¹²C cycles ÷ that: 0.2754 (g1), 2.06 (V). Nucleon (r_p m_p c²/ħc = 3.9977) ÷ 4.1888 = 0.9544.
- Status of those comparisons (corrected wording): both sides are measured or defined standards, and the scheme ambiguity (a factor 7.5 between g1 and V) exceeds the gaps, so 0.2754 vs the valence 0.2748, and 3.9977 vs 4.1888, are undetermined, not matches and not misses. They would become tests only if either side were derived from the floor.
- Weak force: not expressed in a column here. Through the gram: solar neutrinos (Borexino Phase II) cross at 3.46e10 s⁻¹ (pp 3.19e10, ⁷Be 2.61e9, pep 6.6e7); elastic ν–e interactions ≈ 3e-11 s⁻¹ (order of magnitude; recalled sin²θ_W and pp mean energy). The gram's own ¹⁴C makes 0.226 antineutrinos s⁻¹ (natural carbon).

Subordinate understandings
- Three counts of the same gram, each a different level's answer: Bekenstein–Compton 1.0516e38 bits; thermodynamic entropy (diamond, 298.15 K) 2.0709e22 bits; atoms 5.0185e22 (75.41 bits to label one). The first is 15.7 decades above the other two; the thermodynamic and atom counts differ by a factor of 2.4. Entropy source alternatives move it by 2.9%.
- Nothing here derives which count a given level realises; the recursion's job would be how one level's count builds the next.

## Leptons through the gram (subordinate to mass and charge)
Held: 6N electrons (3.01e23). Cosmic muons through the cube face (0.4327 cm²): 0.243–0.257 per minute using the PDG 70 m⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹ (E > 1 GeV) with the 10–15% lower normalization, 0.2855 without, 0.433 for the all-energy "1 cm⁻² min⁻¹" statement (threshold-dependent; carry 0.24–0.43). Hydrogenic radii on the ¹²C nucleus: e 3570.57, μ 17.431, τ 1.1900 r_ch. ¹⁴C betas: one electron and one antineutrino per decay.

## What is identity, observation, open
- Identity: cycles = 1 at Compton; E/E_C = 1/α at Compton; ½ for hydrogen; bits = 2π·cycles/ln 2; the Z_eff·α/2 rung rows.
- Observed, not derived: nucleon r_p ≈ 4ƛ_p; the nucleus at 1.1537 cycles; the 15.7-decade information gap; the neutrino-to-electron step; quark mass share ~1%.
- Open: the weak and colour columns beyond the two schemes above; which language places the levels; a derivation of any ratio from the floor (none here is).
