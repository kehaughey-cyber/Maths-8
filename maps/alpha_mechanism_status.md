## Mechanism candidates
1. **Fixed point of the floor's own flow:** needs a floor renormalization flow with an attractive fixed point; none exists in the repo. (Asymptotic-safety precedent not verified here.)
2. **Induced coupling (Sakharov/Zeldovich):** 1/α_Y(Λ) = 0 with 1/α(0) = (2/3π) Σ N_c Q² ln(Λ/m). With SM content Λ is the Landau pole above (1.4×10²² M_P): not usable without extra charged modes.
3. **Induced + Kaluza–Klein tower (explore/alpha_tower_check.py):** all SM charged content in one dimension, exact threshold counting. KK level coefficients: b̃_Y = 2×20/3 + 1/6 = 27/2, b̃₂ = −7 + 8 + 1/6 = 7/6 (gauge+A₅, Dirac fermions, Higgs). The hypercharge zero sits at the Planck mass for R = 28.41 ℓ_P (27.94 for 1/α_Y(M_P) = 1). The zero falls 28–35 times the tower scale for any tower scale from M_P/12 to M_P/12¹³, so it is set by the content, not by the geometry. The solved R depends on a counting convention: 15.19 (circle without orbifold), 28.41 (orbifold), 54.56 (half the KK content); the input 1/α(m_Z) moves it by 0.3% per 0.2%. The closest named floor number is 30 (Coxeter), 5.6% from 28.41, but R is solved from the measured α and moves by a factor of 2 with convention, so this is not a signal.
4. **SU(2) under the tower (explore/alpha_tower_su2.py):** b̃₂ = +7/6 > 0, so SU(2) screens above 1/R and its coupling can be induced too. With the hypercharge zero at the Planck mass, 1/α₂ falls only to 44.7 there (1/α_em = 44.7); the SU(2) zero needs 276 levels against 28.4, 9.7 times as many. A single common induced scale needs about 16 extra Dirac SU(2) doublets per KK level on top of the SM content. If both couplings vanish at Λ, sin²θ_W(Λ) is a limit of a ratio, not a number.

## Structural conclusion
Above m_Z, α_em is not a single floor number. It is α_Y and α₂ (equivalently α and θ_W): the floor owes two couplings, or one coupling plus θ_W (K2). "The missing piece is one number" holds only at α(0), after the running has already used both.

## Update (2026-10-30, append-only)
**Caveat to the 16-doublet figure (mechanism 4).** It holds hypercharge fixed. Extra SU(2) doublets with Y ≠ 0 also add to b̃_Y and pull the hypercharge zero earlier, so the two conditions must be solved jointly and the count would exceed 16. The figure is independent of hypercharge only for Y = 0 doublets, which carry electric charges ±1/2; fractional-charge searches constrain such states heavily. A common induced scale with this tower therefore needs either exotic half-charged matter or a joint solve needing more than 16 extra doublets.

**Item 1 (does the floor have a compact extra dimension at all?).** As recorded in the spines: no. The ambient dimension is fixed at 3 by the single quaternionic carrier (M5 nodes 42/43, Frobenius/Hurwitz, HELD premise); time is 1-D Lorentzian (node 46); the intrinsic dimension of the substrate is 2 (node 25, a closed surface; the continuum is the 12-cone orbifold sphere, node 52); the substrate is dimensionless with three external anchors ħ, c, G (node 27). The floor's compact objects are the space itself (S³ of unit quaternions, the closed 2-surface) and a finite carrier (120 vertices, finite 2I representations). Nothing in it replicates the charged fields at levels n/R in an extra dimension. So the Kaluza–Klein tower branch (mechanism 3 and 4) is moot unless a premise is added; it should be read as a model of extra charged modes the floor does not supply. Mechanism 2 with floor content alone is the Standard Model run, which fails by 4× (the running check above).

**K2 pointers already on the floor.** θ_W: naive u(2) gives 1/2, refuted (node 57); the native value is not the GUT 3/8 (node 63); the A4/SU(5) embedding in E8 gives the trace ratio 5/3, so a common high-scale coupling gives sin²θ_W = 3/8, but a floating U(1) coupling does not (M6 note on `explore/weinberg_c51_embedding.py`); 1/φ³ = 0.2361 is a fenced candidate for the low-energy value, matched to a measured ratio and not banked (`floor/field_equations_v3.txt` line 274).
