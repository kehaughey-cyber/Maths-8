## The principle (this governs everything)

The only two tests are: **is the math true, and does it match the universe.** Not whether it matches the Standard Model, not convention, not what a previous session was comfortable asserting. The SM, GR and QM are comparison surfaces in the dock, not the bench.

- **No walls.** Archived warnings and failed experiments have a *scope*; they are never general prohibitions. Do not carry forward the Maths 5/6/7 separation walls ("keep mass/frequency, charge labels, content distinct until a map is derived"), the contamination-audit reflex, or any veto on extrapolation. Follow the known paths out from the three touches and do not stop at a wall. If a derivation is true and matches, it stands however unconventional it looks.
- **No fitting.** Never fit a free parameter to a measured value, never introduce a global correction to close a gap, never assume the answer being sought. Exact arithmetic for structural claims.
- **No prediction ceremony.** We are nowhere near an experimental phase; do not stage "predict-then-test" rituals or dress identities as forecasts. State what the math gives and whether it matches.
- **Transmissible.** The repo is the channel (two workers, Claude and Codex, no live link). Persist every substantive result from the repository alone: definitions, the derivation, raw inputs and index/gauge/unit conventions, runnable commands, raw accepted **and** failed outputs, the scoped conclusion, and sha256/commit. A summary is not evidence. Write replays to new files; never overwrite inherited floor or prior evidence.
- **Honesty.** Report raw, unrounded numbers; keep negative controls; correct append-only, naming what was wrong. When something is false or does not match, say so plainly — that is where the value is. Credit inherited results (the floor is Maths 5/6).
- **No preconceptions.** Do not impose spatial or particle pictures; let the math and the observable universe decide the readings. Mass and persistence are not yet established; do not assume them.
