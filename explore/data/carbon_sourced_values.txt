Sourced values for explore/carbon_one_gram.py that are not in a saved data file. Retrieved 2026-10-29 by web search (search-result text, secondary sources; the primary papers were not opened).
1. 12C rms charge radius = 2.4702 fm, uncertainty 0.0022 fm. Source: IAEA Nuclear Charge Radii listing (https://www-nds.iaea.org/radii), which is the Angeli & Marinova table, At. Data Nucl. Data Tables 99 (2013) 69-95. The IAEA page itself is behind a bot check (Cloudflare) and was not opened; the value came from a search result quoting it. Older Mainz electron scattering value 2.464(12) fm.
2. Diamond cubic lattice constant a = 3.56683 angstrom (range 3.56672-3.56684 A at 20-40 C). Source: Straumanis & Aka, J. Am. Chem. Soc. 73 (1951), as quoted in a search result. Diamond cubic cell: 8 atoms, nearest-neighbour distance a*sqrt(3)/4.
3. Graphite in-plane C-C 1.42 A, interlayer 3.35 A, density 2.267 g/cm3 (a = 2.46 A, c = 6.71 A). Search-result quotations, secondary.
4. Sea-level vertical muon intensity (E > 1 GeV) about 0.70e-2 cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1, angular dependence proportional to cos^2(theta). Source: Cosmic ray recipes, arXiv:astro-ph/0601158, as quoted in a search result; mean energy about 4 GeV, most frequent 500 MeV.
5. Modern carbon 14C specific activity 0.226 Bq per gram of carbon (226 Bq/kg). Source: Michigan State Chem 485 lecture notes and Wikipedia "Calculation of radiocarbon dates", as quoted in a search result.
6. 14C half-life 5.70 ky (+-0.03 ky), beta- 100%; 12C abundance 98.94(6)%, 13C 1.06(6)%: NUBASE2020, explore/data/nubase2020_nubase_4.mas20.txt (IAEA AMDC, saved).
7. Diamond S(298.15 K, 1 bar) = 2.3782 J/(mol K) (DeSorbo 1953), Cp(298.16 K) = 6.1149 J/(mol K) (DeSorbo 1953): NIST WebBook condensed-phase page saved as explore/data/nist_webbook_diamond_condensed.html.
